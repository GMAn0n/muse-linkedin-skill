LinkedIn skill for Muse

Drop-in LinkedIn skill for Meta's Muse AI: publish posts, read your profile, and list recent posts through a secure token-based connector.

Download linkedin-skill.zip

The package contains the generalized skill (linkedin/ folder), a setup guide (SETUP.md), and API notes. No credentials included — you bring your own access token.

What you need (no client ID, no client secret)

Just one thing: your own LinkedIn access token. The skill never needs anyone's client ID or client secret — those stay with whoever owns the LinkedIn developer app.

  1. Go to the LinkedIn Developer Portal and open (or create) an app.
  2. Under Products, enable what you want the skill to do:
    • Share on LinkedIn → lets the skill publish posts (w_member_social)
    • Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect → lets the skill read your profile (openid profile email)
  3. Use the portal's token generator to mint an access token with those scopes. Copy it.
  4. Copy the linkedin/ folder from the zip into your Muse workspace at ~/workspace/skills/ and store your access token in your platform's custom connector as a Bearer on api.linkedin.com.
Rules of the road: the post command always requires your explicit approval of the exact text before anything publishes. Tokens last about 60 days — re-mint in the portal when it expires. Listing your own recent posts (posts) needs a partner-restricted product; without it the command reports "not approved" instead of failing silently.
Commands: profile · post · posts  |  Permanent mirror — no expiring links.
