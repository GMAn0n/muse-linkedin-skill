SETUP

From download to first profile read.

You need your own LinkedIn developer app and access token. The skill itself never needs your app’s client ID or secret.

Mint your access token

Open your app in the LinkedIn Developer Portal. Enable Share on LinkedIn for posting and Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect for profile reads, then use the portal’s token generator.

Install the folder

Unzip the package and copy linkedin/ into ~/workspace/skills/linkedin/ in your Muse workspace.

Connect securely in Muse

Ask Muse to “set up a LinkedIn connector,” then enter your access token in the secure form—not in chat. Try “read my LinkedIn profile” when connected.
